Privacy Policy for Paper Tankies

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Paper Tankies is developed by Sergey Ryabtsev.

Data Collection

Paper Tankies does not collect or transmit personal data. The game does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting services, user accounts, developer-operated servers, Game Center, or connected third-party services.

App Store Purchases

Paper Tankies is purchased through the Apple App Store. Apple processes payments under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support.

Children’s Privacy

The game does not collect personal data from children or other players.

Changes

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated.
